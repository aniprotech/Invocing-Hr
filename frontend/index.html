Getting started

How to use aniprotech

Four steps from signing up to being paid. Nothing to install on a server, and no card needed to look around.

1

Create your account

Sign up with your email and a password. We send a code to that address and you enter it once, so the address on your invoices is one you actually own.

2

Set up your business

Your logo, address, currency and tax rates — they flow onto every document.

  • Send through your own Gmail, or your own mail server
  • Connect a payment provider to collect card and bank payments
3

Add your people and customers

Employees, departments, contacts and items. Staff get their own portal for attendance, leave and payslips.

4

Send it and get paid

Raise an invoice or a quote, send it, and watch what happens to it. Anything that fails to leave is listed rather than quietly marked as sent.

Install

Put aniprotech on your device

There is nothing to download from an app store. It installs from your browser in a few taps and then opens full screen, with its own icon, like any other app.

aniprotech

Add to Home Screen
Add to Favourites

Staff install a different app. Employees should open the employee portal first and install from there. It arrives as its own icon, opens straight into clocking in, leave and payslips, and does not need an account on this side.

  1. Open this site in Safari. Chrome and Firefox on an iPhone cannot do this properly.
  2. Tap the Share button — the square with an arrow coming out of it.
  3. Scroll down the list and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Give it a name and tap Add.

You will be asked to sign in again inside the installed app. iOS keeps a home-screen app's sign-in separate from Safari's, so this happens once and then it remembers you.

  1. Open this site in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three dots at the top right.
  3. Tap Install app, or Add to Home screen on older versions.
  4. Confirm with Install.

Chrome often offers this by itself with a banner at the bottom of the screen. Taking the banner does the same thing.

  1. Chrome or Edge: look for the install icon at the right-hand end of the address bar — a screen with a downward arrow — and click Install.
  2. No icon? Open the browser menu and choose Install this site as an app, or Apps → Install this site.
  3. Safari on a Mac: choose File, then Add to Dock.

It opens in its own window without tabs or an address bar, and it lands in your Start menu, Launchpad or Dock like anything else.
