                                    through us.

API keys

                                
                            

                            

                                

                                    Send it as Authorization: Bearer ak_.... Read keys get
                                    GET /api/v1/employees, /api/v1/employees/{id},
                                    /api/v1/departments, /api/v1/leave?status=approved&since=YYYY-MM-DD

Webhooks

                                
                            

                            

                                

                                    A JSON POST to your https URL for each event, with X-Aniprotech-Event,
                                    X-Aniprotech-Delivery and X-Aniprotech-Signature: sha256=<HMAC of the body with your secret>.
                                    A non-2xx answer is retried after 1, 5, 30 and 120 minutes; twenty failures in a row switch it off.

 Geofence & Work Hours

                            

                                

                                    

                                    

                                    

                                    

                                    

                                    

                                    

                                    
                                    

                                        
                                        
                                        
                                            What a full day has to add up to. Short days, full days and
                                            overtime are all judged against this, and it is the target on
                                            the dial staff see in their portal.
                                        
                                    

                                    

                                        
                                        
                                        
                                            Anything below this counts as a half day rather than a short one.
                                        
                                    

                                    

                                    

                                    

                                        
                                        
                                        
                                        
                                    

                                

                                

                                    
                                    

                                        
                                        
                                        
                                        
                                        
                                        
                                        
                                    

                                    
                                    

                                        Leave this off and staff clock in themselves. Either way, signing in on a
                                        non-working day never records attendance — so someone opening the portal
                                        on a Sunday to read a payslip or upload a document is not marked present.
                                        They can still clock in by hand if they really are working.
