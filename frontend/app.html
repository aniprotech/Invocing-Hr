Geofence & Work Hours

What a full day has to add up to. Short days, full days and overtime are all judged against this, and it is the target on the dial staff see in their portal.
Anything below this counts as a half day rather than a short one.

Leave this off and staff clock in themselves. Either way, signing in on a non-working day never records attendance — so someone opening the portal on a Sunday to read a payslip or upload a document is not marked present. They can still clock in by hand if they really are working.
