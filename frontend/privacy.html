Privacy Policy

Last updated 5 September 2026

aniprotech is business software for invoicing, HR, payroll and attendance. This policy explains what it stores, why, who else sees it, and how to have it removed. It covers the aniprotech website and the application at aniprotech.com.

Before publishing: replace this box with your registered company name, company number and postal address. A privacy policy has to say who the data controller is, and that is a fact only you can supply.

1. Who this policy is about

Two different groups use aniprotech, and the distinction matters:

  • Businesses that sign up and use the software. For their own account data, we are the data controller.
  • Employees and customers of those businesses, whose details a business enters into the software. For that data we are a processor: the business decides what to store and why, and we hold it on their instruction. If you are an employee asking about your record, please ask your employer first, as they control it.

2. What we store

When a business signs up

  • Email address and a password, stored only as a one-way hash
  • Company name, contact name, phone number, address, website and tax or business number, where entered
  • Logo, currency and industry, where entered
  • A six-digit code emailed once to confirm the address is reachable

What a business puts in

  • Customers: name, email, address and the invoices, quotes and payments raised against them
  • Employees: name, work email, phone, address, job title, salary or hourly rate, tax rate, allowances and deductions, bank name and account details, and tax identifier — entered by the employer for payroll
  • Attendance: clock-in and clock-out times, hours, breaks, and — only where the employer has switched location checking on and the person allows it in their browser — the coordinates at the moment of clocking in
  • Leave requests, documents, appraisals, onboarding tasks and anything else typed into the product

Automatically, as the software runs

  • Sign-in records: the email used, the time, the IP address, the browser's own description of itself, and whether the attempt succeeded. These exist so an account holder can see who has been signing in.
  • A record of each email the system sends, including whether it was delivered and any error returned
  • Wallet transactions: what was charged, for which action and when

There is no advertising, tracking or analytics on this site. aniprotech loads no analytics script, no advertising pixel and no third-party tracker on any page. We do not build profiles, we do not track people across other websites, and we do not sell or rent personal data to anybody, ever.

3. Cookies

One cookie: a signed session cookie set when you sign in, so the application knows it is still you between requests. It is marked SameSite=Lax, sent only over HTTPS, and expires after 24 hours by default. It holds a reference to your session and no personal information.

The application also uses your browser's own local storage to remember small conveniences, such as a collapsed panel or a dismissed message. That never leaves your device.

There are no advertising or analytics cookies, so there is nothing to consent to.

4. Google account data

A business may connect a Google account so its invoices and payslips are sent from its own address rather than ours. This is optional, and the software works without it.

When connected, we request these permissions and no others:

PermissionWhat we do with it
openid, email, profile Identify the account being connected, and show its address on the settings screen so you can see which one is in use.
gmail.send Send the invoices, quotes and payslips that you choose to send, from your own address. Nothing else.

We cannot read your email. The gmail.send permission only allows sending. We do not request, and could not use, any permission that reads, searches, modifies or deletes messages in your mailbox.

Google issues a token that lets the software keep sending on your behalf. It is stored on our server, is never shown in any web page, and is deleted immediately when you press Disconnect on the settings screen or delete your account.

Limited Use. aniprotech's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically: data obtained through Google APIs is used only to provide the sending feature described above. It is not transferred to anyone else except as needed to provide that feature, to comply with law, or as part of a merger or acquisition. It is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not read by any person except where you have specifically asked us to investigate a problem or where the law requires it. No Google user data is used to train any machine learning or AI model.

Only these, and only for the part of the job each one does. None of them is permitted to use the data for their own purposes.

We do not sell personal data, we do not share it for advertising, and we do not give it to anyone else unless the law requires it.

6. Where it is kept and for how long

Before publishing: state the region your Railway database runs in, and how long you keep data after an account is closed. We suggest 30 days and then permanent deletion, but the number has to be one you will actually honour.

While an account is open, data is kept for as long as the business needs it — invoices and payroll records usually have to be retained for tax purposes, and that period is set by the law where the business operates, not by us.

When an account is closed, its data is deleted after the period stated above. Sign-in records and email delivery records are kept for up to 12 months so that security questions can be answered, then removed.

7. How it is protected

  • Every connection uses HTTPS
  • Passwords are stored as one-way hashes and never in a readable form
  • Payment keys, mail passwords and Google tokens are stored server-side and are never returned to a web page, even to an administrator
  • Each business's data is separated by account, and every request that touches it is checked against the account it belongs to
  • Signing in starts a fresh session, so a session cannot be handed to somebody else

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data we will tell you and, where the law requires it, the relevant authority.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have the right to ask for a copy of your data, to correct it, to have it deleted, to restrict or object to how it is used, and to receive it in a portable form. To exercise any of these, write to the address below. We will respond within 30 days.

If you are an employee or a customer of a business using aniprotech, that business controls your record. Please contact them first; if you cannot reach them, contact us and we will help.

You can also ask us to delete your account at any time, and you can disconnect a Google account yourself at any time from the settings screen. You can revoke our access directly from your Google account permissions page.

9. Children

aniprotech is business software and is not intended for anybody under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top and, for anything significant, tell account holders by email.
